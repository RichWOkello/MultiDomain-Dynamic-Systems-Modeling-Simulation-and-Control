PHYSICAL DOMAINS

One control framework, multiple systems.

Each notebook starts from physical laws, builds a mathematical model, simulates the dynamics and applies control-system analysis.

Mechanical

Mass–spring–damper, rotational systems, gears, multi-DOF models, friction, backlash and saturation.

Electrical

RC/RL/RLC circuits, DC motors, power converters, transformers, generators and coupled electromechanics.

P

Pneumatic

Compressible-air cylinder dynamics, valve flow, chamber pressure, leakage, spool dynamics and load motion.

Hydraulic

Fluid compressibility, orifice flow, asymmetric cylinders, relief behavior, accumulators and cavitation logic.
